# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a respectful and welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Showing empathy and respect toward other participants.
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.
- Taking responsibility and apologizing when mistakes are made.
- Respecting differing opinions and experiences.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include harassment, intimidation, personal attacks, discrimination, sexualized language or imagery, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate it. Maintainers may take proportionate corrective action in response to behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the repository owner through the contact method on the owner's GitHub profile. Do not report urgent safety issues in public issues.
